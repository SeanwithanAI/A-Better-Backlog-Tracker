Connect a game database

Searching uses RAWG, a free game database with user ratings, genres, screenshots and descriptions. It needs a free API key (takes about a minute):

  1. Go to rawg.io/apidocs and click Get API Key.
  2. Make a free account and fill in the short form (any app name and URL work).
  3. Copy the key and paste it here.

Steam playtime

Pulls your Steam library and playtime. Each sync compares against the last one and adds the new hours to your play log automatically. Backlog games you've started playing move to Playing.

Get a key at steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey (any domain name works). Your SteamID64 is the long number in your profile link, or look it up at steamid.io. In Steam's privacy settings, Game details must be Public.

Steam doesn't let web pages read its data directly. With a proxy, Sync now works in one tap. Without one, use the two-step method below.
